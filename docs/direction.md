# Kage no Machi direction

*Kage no Machi* is an isometric Edo city builder running on Flutter `3.50.1`, `flutter_scene` (pinned at `d04d5cc5`), `flutter_scene_soloud`, and macOS Metal. It ships two playable Edo showcase realms — **Shukuba Valley** (riverside highway post-town, or *shukuba*) and **Minato-jō** (*港城*, coastal castle & harbor port), switchable from the title menu, HUD, or `--dart-define=SHUKUBA_REALM=minato_jo` — shaped from golden dusk into blue-hour night while every road, garden path, stone terrace, wall, rock outcrop, cottage, shop, shrine, and tree responds with spatial pentatonic instruments.

## Visual commitment to a fine-detail painted diorama

*Kage no Machi* commits to Style B: **Warm Sunset Gouache Dusk + Luminous Blue-Hour Night + *Urushi* & *Washi* HUD**, combining seamless hand-painted gouache textures and botanical sprays anchored in `docs/references/midjourney/` with Blender 5.3.0 hero `.glb` landmarks, `docs/references/shadow-tactics/st-01.jpg` through `st-25.jpg`, and historical Azuchi-Momoyama / early Edo village carpentry.

1. **Human-scale four-sided Edo carpentry, structural building variations, sculpted hero `.glb` landmarks, and Midjourney botanical sprays.** Dwellings scale around `1.66 m` townsfolk with `1.8 m` doorway lintels and `3.1 m` to `4.5 m` roof ridges. Sculpted Blender 5.3.0 `.glb` hero assets (`assets/models/edo_*.glb`, built via `tools/blender/build_all.py`) anchor the arched *ran-kan* vermilion bridge, upturned *sori* *torii* gate, 5-tier *Gojū-no-tō* hilltop pagoda, *Kasuga-dōrō* stone lanterns, riverside *kawa-kaidan* stairs (`edo_river_stairs.glb`), cargo *niguruma* handcarts, *wagasa* tea benches, *nobori* banners, and *komodaru* sake casks alongside the castle & port kit (`castle_tenshukaku.glb`, `castle_yagura.glb`, `castle_otemon_gate.glb`, `castle_ishigaki_*.glb`, `castle_hei_wall.glb`, `harbor_pier.glb`, `harbor_todai.glb`, `harbor_crane.glb`, `ship_sengokubune.glb`, `boat_wasen.glb`, `harbor_cargo.glb`), sculpted craggy `minato_coastal_islands.glb`, smooth C^1 multi-peak `vista_mountains.glb`, organic rock outcrops (`rock_cliff_a/b.glb`, `rock_river_boulder.glb`, `rock_shore_riprap.glb`, `rock_coastal_headland.glb`), and baked dark-cedar *kumiko* shoji window lattices (`ShojiGlow`). Multi-variant hero buildings (`machiya_hero*.glb`, `kura_warehouse_hero*.glb`, `teahouse_hero*.glb`, `minka_hero_v*.glb`, `yatai_stall_v*.glb`) feature watertight non-intersecting post-and-beam framing, proud *noren-kake* lintels, and clean split *namako*/*plaster* vaults. Volumetric 4-shell crossed-card pine tufts (`tree_pine_bonsai.glb`, `IslandPine_Canopy`) and Midjourney seamless surface textures (`assets/textures/*.png`) + painterly foliage sprites (`foliage_maple`, `foliage_willow`, `foliage_pine`, `foliage_bamboo`) bind through [`gouache_lit.fmat`](../assets/materials/gouache_lit.fmat) and [`foliage_canopy.fmat`](../assets/materials/foliage_canopy.fmat) on towering `7.8 m` to `13.5 m` trees, while the outer countryside apron splits into 8 seamless ring sectors outside `[-88, 88]`. Repeated placements cycle across four *minka* farmhouse silhouettes, four *machiya* shop silhouettes, three *chaya* teahouses, three *kura* storehouses, and four *yatai* market stalls, advancing automatically on placement or when you press `V`.
2. **Unified physical sky IBL, Style B golden-hour contrast, shadow-map *komorebi* god rays, and clustered street lanterns.** [`EdoLookDriver`](../lib/shukuba/look/edo_look_driver.dart) pairs `PhysicalSkySource`, `SkyEnvironment`, and `Skybox` with a single 4-cascade `DirectionalLight` using `DirectionalShadowFilter.pcss` across `145 m` (with screen-space contact shadows kept disabled so fp16 linear depth does not stripe distant `100 m–250 m` ridges). Balanced golden dusk lighting (`environmentIntensity: 2.55`, `exposure: 1.02`, lifted dusk `groundColor`) contrasts warm sunlit roofs and glowing *chōchin* lanterns against cool Prussian-indigo shadows (`shadowAmbientStrength` in `[0.28, 0.36]`, wide bent-normal GTAO `intensity: 1.15`, `radius: 1.25`, `power: 1.48`) while transitioning smoothly into luminous Blue-Hour Night (`environmentIntensity: 1.45`, `exposure: 0.98`, zero pitch-black voids). Sixty-four-step volumetric `GodRaysPass` ray-marching (`54 m`), [`sumi_ink.frag`](../shaders/sumi_ink.frag) depth-gated mini-Kuwahara gouache brushwork (`kuwaharaMix = 0.14`) with horizon-masked smooth-attenuating sumi ink contours (`smoothstep(0.24, 0.78, scaledCrease)`), and fine *washi* paper tooth finish the painterly look alongside [`AtmosphereBuilder`](../lib/shukuba/world/atmosphere_builder.dart).
3. **Mip-filtered parallax relief, organic feathered shorelines, blended road junctions, lush `4 x 4` flora chunks, and refractive caustics water.** [`gouache_lit.fmat`](../assets/materials/gouache_lit.fmat) computes analytical `fwidth` mip weights, marches a 4-step view-ray heightfield in `SampleReliefHeight`, evaluates 2-tap directional micro-horizon self-shadowing, and writes cavity darkening alongside wide bent-normal GTAO and `SamplePaintedLinear` Midjourney surface textures. [`TerrainBuilder`](../lib/shukuba/world/terrain_builder.dart) encodes longitudinal tip feathering and road-to-path junction weights into `GetUV1`, shapes non-polygonal organic riverbank/coastline transitions while keeping engineered harbor quays and castle stone cliffs crisp, [`FloraScatterBuilder`](../lib/shukuba/world/flora_scatter_builder.dart) scatters meadow ground cover in `4 x 4` chunks through [`living_meadow.fmat`](../assets/materials/living_meadow.fmat), [`river_water.fmat`](../assets/materials/river_water.fmat) samples `scene_color` and `scene_depth` with `GetSceneWorldPosition` for jade-teal Gerstner refraction, soft flow-elongated FBM current wisps, and widened `0.40 m` feathered shorelines, and [`harbor_water.fmat`](../assets/materials/harbor_water.fmat) renders organic domain-warped bay swells across a `±560 m` extended ocean grid with a seamless circular horizon blend (`320–520 m`).
4. **Sculpted multi-tier elevation, *urushi* & *washi* HUD shell, incremental entity caching, and valley-focal audio.** Terrain uses a `0.5 m` contour-aligned mesh tiled across a `4 x 4` chunk grid spanning `176 m x 176 m` with *tanada* paddies, *mitsuke* checkpoints, *ishigaki* walls, and walkable *kaidan* staircases. Startup paints the framed *Kage no Machi* **Title Menu** over a painted dusk backdrop (`assets/ui/title_backdrop.jpg`), hammered antique-brass maple crest (`assets/ui/urushi_crest.png`), falling *momiji*/*ichō* leaves, and *kinpaku* gold flecks ([`shukuba_shell_overlays.dart`](../lib/shukuba/view/shukuba_shell_overlays.dart)) while audio prewarm runs in a background isolate. [`ShukubaHud`](../lib/shukuba/view/shukuba_hud.dart) keeps over ninety percent of the viewport open with deep *urushi* lacquer & hammered-brass header pills (`44 px`), `32 px` lacquered dock tabs, and `42 px` cream *washi* tool cards showcasing Midjourney woodblock architectural artwork (`assets/ui/icon_*.png`) that glow cinnabar-amber when selected. [`EdoAudioDriver`](../lib/shukuba/audio/edo_audio_driver.dart) mounts `SoloudAudioEngine` with valley-focal spatial pentatonic audio, while [`EdoWorldDriver`](../lib/shukuba/world/edo_world_driver.dart) caches unchanged structure `Node`s and mounts hero `.glb` landmarks cleanly.

## Parked systems for watchtowers and stealth intrigue

Watchtowers, Magistrate's Eye sightcone decals, and nighttime smuggling mechanics remain parked while work focuses on tactile Edo city building and painted diorama craft. Low-level `DecalNode` shader support stays in the codebase for future use, while watchtowers and vision-cone overlays remain out of the active HUD, starter ward, and player toolset.

## Visual evaluation loop

Every rendering, shader, or geometry change follows a closed Metal screenshot loop:

1. Capture settled `1440x778` frames for both **Shukuba Valley** and **Minato-jō** (`--dart-define=SHUKUBA_REALM=minato_jo`), plus the 10-angle `SHUKUBA_AUDIT_360=true` orbital sweep (34 total presets across both realms), 4-corner building turntables, ten close-up geometry-seam cameras, and live UI screenshots via `SHUKUBA_CAPTURE_ATLAS=true` with [`ShukubaFrameHarness`](../lib/shukuba/verify/frame_harness.dart). On time-of-day transitions, the harness pumps `26` frames so `SkyEnvironment` finishes its time-sliced cubemap, prefilter, and spherical-harmonics bake before reading pixels, while unchanged time-of-day shots pump `2` frames. Confirm zero coplanar overlaps, zero perturbed depth conflicts, and zero engine punctual-light or near-plane warnings across all capture runs.
2. Inspect the captured PNGs directly side by side against `docs/references/shadow-tactics/st-01.jpg` through `st-25.jpg`.
3. Check house-to-person proportions, structural variation across repeated buildings, blended road-to-path and *tanada* junctions, `flora_tile_r_c` ground overgrowth and countryside groves, mip-filtered parallax relief, refractive riverbed caustics, crisp close-up foreground clarity, nocturnal Prussian-indigo roof luminance, and calm HUD legibility before accepting the change.

## Model roles

Work in this repository follows strict model ownership:

- **Carbon** owns all 3D geometry, `.fmat` and post-processing shaders, visual evaluation against the reference corpus, UX design, deep research, and orchestration.
- **Argon** assists with bounded systems plumbing, unit tests, and static or runtime verification, always reviewed and visually verified by Carbon.
- **Flash 3.8** handles fast file search and reference retrieval.
