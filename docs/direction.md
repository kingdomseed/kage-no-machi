# Kage no Machi direction

*Kage no Machi* is an atmospheric isometric Edo post-town builder running on Flutter `3.50.0`, `flutter_scene` `0.24.3`, `flutter_scene_soloud` `0.2.0`, and macOS Metal. You shape a riverside highway post-town, or *shukuba*, from golden dusk into blue-hour night while every road, garden path, stone terrace, wall, rock outcrop, cottage, shop, shrine, and tree responds with spatial pentatonic instruments.

## Visual commitment to fine-detail painted diorama (Direction B)

*Kage no Machi* commits to **Direction B**, a fine-detail painted diorama built procedurally in code rather than a flat low-poly toy look. Visual scale, surface weathering, and composition anchor on `docs/references/shadow-tactics/st-01.jpg` through `st-25.jpg` and historical Azuchi-Momoyama and early Edo village carpentry.

1. **Human-scale 4-sided Edo carpentry, structural building variations, and towering botanical contrast.** Dwellings scale intimately around `1.66 m` townsfolk with `1.8 m` doorway lintels and `3.1 m` to `4.5 m` roof ridges, while jointed bamboo groves (`9.5 m` to `13.5 m`), black pines, *sugi* cedars, and autumn maples (`7.8 m` to `11.2 m`) rise two to three times higher overhead. Every building frames all four exterior elevations with post-and-beam bays (*hashira* and *nuki*), horizontal *shitami-ita* weatherboarding, *tobukuro* shutter boxes, hooded side windows, rear service doors, *mushiko-mado* loft grilles, and cut-stone *kutsunugi-ishi* threshold steps. Repeated placements vary structurally across four *minka* farmhouse silhouettes (*kayabuki* thatch, L-plan *magariya*, stone-shingle cottage, and *hisashi* homestead), four *machiya* shop silhouettes (*kawara* merchant, *hoshidai* loft, *udatsu* dyer shop, and *nurigome* apothecary), three *chaya* teahouses, three *kura* storehouses, and four *yatai* market stalls, advancing automatically on placement or when pressing `V`.
2. **Full-valley habitat flora, fractured layered stone, and churned dirt.** Roughly three-quarters of the `176 m x 176 m` valley floor stays calm, richly textured earth, damp moss, wheel-rutted ochre clay, embedded pebbles, and low-roughness puddles in [`TerrainBuilder`](../lib/shukuba/world/terrain_builder.dart) and [`gouache_lit.fmat`](../assets/materials/gouache_lit.fmat). Natural *iwa* rock outcrops stay at grounded outcrop heights (`1.4 m` to `2.6 m`) with deep cellular fractures, warm sunlit ochre crowns, cool slate shadow bellies, and mossy pockets. [`FloraScatterBuilder`](../lib/shukuba/world/flora_scatter_builder.dart) scatters 3D grass tufts, *susuki* plumes, *nogiku* wildflowers, crimson *higanbana*, wall drapes, and riverside *ashi* reeds across `-85 m` to `+85 m` into habitat pockets hugging walls, boulders, tree skirts, *tanada* bunds, and water margins through [`living_meadow.fmat`](../assets/materials/living_meadow.fmat).
3. **Sculpted multi-tier elevation, contour-graded *tanada*, seamless stone junctions, and street festival life.** Terrain uses a `0.5 m` contour-aligned mesh tiled across a `4 x 4` spatial chunk grid spanning `176 m x 176 m` so riverbanks, berms, six-tier contour-graded *tanada* rice paddies (`0.44 m` to `0.54 m` gentle steps with wooden *hi-no-kuchi* spillways), *mitsuke* highway checkpoints, and retained [`TerraceCourse`](../lib/shukuba/model/shukuba_types.dart) plateaus grade smoothly into battered *ishigaki* walls, *sangi-zumi* corner quoins, and walkable *kaidan* staircases with flush cut-stone *shikishi* landing aprons. Low shadow ambient fill and wide GTAO ground every overhang in cool Prussian-indigo shadow pools. Market plazas and temple terraces gather striped, wheeled, and *wagasa*-shaded *yatai* stalls, overhead *fukinagashi* festival streamers on catenary cords, wooden water tubs, plank duckboards, roofed *ido* courtyard wells, raked *karesansui* gravel courts, and a timber *shōrō* bell pavilion.
4. **Own *Kage no Machi* woodblock shell, calm progressive-disclosure HUD, phased boot telemetry, and dirty-tile chunking.** Startup paints the *Kage no Machi* (`影の町`) woodblock and *urushi* lacquer Title Menu immediately with live audio settings and field notes while WAV synthesis runs on a background isolate, then advances through a stage-timed Loading Curtain and hidden GPU pipeline warmup before revealing the valley. In the valley, `ShukubaHud` keeps over ninety percent of the diorama unobstructed by default with compact top pills (`Views` presets `1` through `5`, `Menu`, dusk-to-night slider, and mute) and a single-drawer bottom dock that opens one tool category at a time. Editing a road, terrace, wall, or building invalidates only intersecting `44 m x 44 m` terrain and flora chunks.

## Parked systems for watchtowers and stealth intrigue

Watchtowers, Magistrate's Eye sightcone decals, and nighttime smuggling mechanics remain parked while we finish visual craft, UI polish, and valley expansion. Low-level `DecalNode` shader support stays in the repository for future use, while watchtowers and vision-cone overlays remain out of the active HUD, starter ward, and player toolset.

## Visual evaluation loop

Every rendering, shader, or geometry change follows a closed Metal screenshot loop:

1. Capture settled `1440x778` frames across all six valley presets with `ShukubaFrameHarness`, plus multi-angle 4-corner building turntables, ten close-up geometry-seam cameras, and live UI screenshots via `SHUKUBA_CAPTURE_ATLAS=true`, confirming zero coplanar overlaps and zero perturbed depth conflicts across every shot.
2. Inspect the captured PNGs directly side by side against `docs/references/shadow-tactics/st-01.jpg` through `st-25.jpg`.
3. Check house-to-person proportions, structural variation across repeated buildings, seamless road-to-staircase and *tanada* junctions, full-valley habitat foliage, fractured rock and rutted dirt normal relief, shadow depth, and calm HUD legibility before accepting the change.
