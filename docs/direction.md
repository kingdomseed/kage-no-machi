# Kage no Machi direction

*Kage no Machi* is an isometric Edo post-town builder running on Flutter `3.50.0`, `flutter_scene` `0.24.3`, `flutter_scene_soloud` `0.2.0`, and macOS Metal. You shape a riverside highway post-town, or *shukuba*, from golden dusk into blue-hour night while every road, garden path, stone terrace, wall, rock outcrop, cottage, shop, shrine, and tree responds with spatial pentatonic instruments.

## Visual commitment to a fine-detail painted diorama

*Kage no Machi* commits to Direction B, a fine-detail painted diorama built procedurally in code rather than a flat low-poly toy look. Visual scale, surface weathering, and lighting composition anchor on `docs/references/shadow-tactics/st-01.jpg` through `st-25.jpg` and historical Azuchi-Momoyama and early Edo village carpentry.

1. **Human-scale four-sided Edo carpentry, structural building variations, and towering trees.** Dwellings scale around `1.66 m` townsfolk with `1.8 m` doorway lintels and `3.1 m` to `4.5 m` roof ridges. Jointed bamboo groves from `9.5 m` to `13.5 m`, black pines, *sugi* cedars, weeping willows, and autumn maples from `7.8 m` to `11.2 m` rise two to three times higher overhead, including 34 outer countryside groves and rock spurs beyond `25 m`. Every building frames all four exterior elevations with *hashira* posts, *nuki* tie beams, horizontal *shitami-ita* cedar siding, *tobukuro* shutter boxes, hooded side windows, rear service doors, *mushiko-mado* loft grilles, and cut-stone *kutsunugi-ishi* threshold steps. Repeated placements cycle across four *minka* farmhouse silhouettes, four *machiya* shop silhouettes, three *chaya* teahouses, three *kura* storehouses, and four *yatai* market stalls, advancing automatically on placement or when you press `V`.
2. **Unified physical sky IBL, soft PCSS shadows, shadow-map *komorebi* god rays, and clustered street lanterns.** [`EdoLookDriver`](../lib/shukuba/look/edo_look_driver.dart) pairs `PhysicalSkySource`, `SkyEnvironment`, and `Skybox` with a single 4-cascade `DirectionalLight` using `DirectionalShadowFilter.pcss` across `145 m` and screen-space contact shadows. Both the sky dome and key light share `TimeOfDayRig.celestialSkyDirection` so the glowing solar disk (`18.5` down to `10.0` degrees during dusk, `16.1` degrees at `goldenDusk`) and nocturnal silver moon disk (`18` to `30` degrees) stay locked opposite `DirectionalLight.direction`. Sixty-four-step volumetric `GodRaysPass` ray-marching bounded to `54 m` inside the shadow cascades streams crepuscular shafts radially from the sun through perforated tree crowns, replacing flat per-tree curtains. After `timeOfDay = 0.55`, the key light switches to cool moonlight while `EdoLookDriver.clusterLanternOrigins` merges street and building *chōchin* anchors within `5.5 m` into at most `12` weighted `PointLight` clusters with an `8.2 m` range. [`AtmosphereBuilder`](../lib/shukuba/world/atmosphere_builder.dart) adds depth-softened river mist, alley haze, chimney steam wisps, lantern halos, and firefly motes. Exponential height fog starts at `18 m` at dusk and `22 m` at night so low close-up sightlines stay crisp in the foreground while distant ridges recede into atmospheric haze.
3. **Mip-filtered parallax relief, blended road junctions, lush `4 x 4` flora chunks, and refractive caustics water.** [`gouache_lit.fmat`](../assets/materials/gouache_lit.fmat) computes analytical `fwidth` mip weights, marches a 4-step view-ray heightfield in `SampleReliefHeight`, evaluates 2-tap directional micro-horizon self-shadowing, and writes cavity darkening into `material.occlusion` alongside wide bent-normal GTAO. [`TerrainBuilder`](../lib/shukuba/world/terrain_builder.dart) encodes longitudinal tip feathering and road-to-path junction weights into `GetUV1` so *kaidō* highways and boulder-free *roji* garden paths cross-blend cleanly at shared endpoints and feather into surrounding soil. [`FloraScatterBuilder`](../lib/shukuba/world/flora_scatter_builder.dart) partitions ground vegetation into sixteen `44 m x 44 m` `flora_tile_r_c` spatial chunks and scatters 9-frond clover, fern, and moss overgrowth, grass tufts, *susuki* plumes, *nogiku* wildflowers, *higanbana* lilies, wall drapes, and riverside *ashi* reeds through [`living_meadow.fmat`](../assets/materials/living_meadow.fmat). [`river_water.fmat`](../assets/materials/river_water.fmat) samples `scene_color` and `scene_depth` with `GetSceneWorldPosition` to render Gerstner wave refraction, Beer-Lambert depth absorption, and dual-layer cellular riverbed caustics.
4. **Sculpted multi-tier elevation, contour-graded *tanada*, woodblock HUD shell, incremental entity caching, and valley-focal audio.** Terrain uses a `0.5 m` contour-aligned mesh tiled across a `4 x 4` chunk grid spanning `176 m x 176 m` so riverbanks, six-tier contour-graded *tanada* rice paddies, *mitsuke* highway checkpoints, and retained [`TerraceCourse`](../lib/shukuba/model/shukuba_types.dart) plateaus grade smoothly into battered *ishigaki* walls, *sangi-zumi* corner quoins, and walkable *kaidan* staircases with flush *shikishi* landing aprons. Startup paints the *Kage no Machi* woodblock and *urushi* lacquer **Title Menu** immediately while WAV synthesis runs on a background isolate, then advances through a stage-timed **Loading Curtain** and GPU warmup before revealing the valley. [`EdoAudioDriver`](../lib/shukuba/audio/edo_audio_driver.dart) mounts `SoloudAudioEngine` with a valley-focal `AudioListener` (`edo_audio_listener`) tracking `PerspectiveCamera.target` at zoom-scaled ear height (`3.5 m` to `20.6 m`) so *kaidō* river, wind, and cricket loops plus spatial pentatonic placement instruments (*koto*, *shakuhachi*, *hyōshigi*, *bonshō*) play clearly across isometric overview and close-up presets without Doppler pitch warp, and tear down cleanly when returning to the **Title Menu**. `ShukubaHud` keeps over ninety percent of the viewport unobstructed with compact top pills for **Views** presets `1` through `6`, **Menu**, the dusk-to-night slider, and mute, plus a single-drawer bottom dock, while [`TacticalCameraRig`](../lib/shukuba/view/tactical_camera_rig.dart) tilts smoothly down to `2` degrees to frame the horizon sun and canopy shafts. [`EdoWorldDriver`](../lib/shukuba/world/edo_world_driver.dart) caches unchanged structure `Node`s by `EntityId` alongside roads, terraces, walls, spans, and agents so placing a building rebuilds only the new structure plus intersecting `44 m x 44 m` terrain and `flora_tile_r_c` chunks while preserving `shadowStatic` caches everywhere else.

## Parked systems for watchtowers and stealth intrigue

Watchtowers, Magistrate's Eye sightcone decals, and nighttime smuggling mechanics remain parked while work focuses on tactile Edo city building and painted diorama craft. Low-level `DecalNode` shader support stays in the codebase for future use, while watchtowers and vision-cone overlays remain out of the active HUD, starter ward, and player toolset.

## Visual evaluation loop

Every rendering, shader, or geometry change follows a closed Metal screenshot loop:

1. Capture settled `1440x778` frames across all six valley presets plus `horizon_sun_canopy` and `horizon_hillside_tilt` with [`ShukubaFrameHarness`](../lib/shukuba/verify/frame_harness.dart), plus 4-corner building turntables, ten close-up geometry-seam cameras, and live UI screenshots via `SHUKUBA_CAPTURE_ATLAS=true`. On time-of-day transitions, the harness pumps `18` frames so `SkyEnvironment` finishes its 16-step time-sliced cubemap, prefilter, and spherical-harmonics bake before reading pixels, while unchanged time-of-day shots pump `2` frames. Confirm zero coplanar overlaps, zero perturbed depth conflicts, and zero engine punctual-light or near-plane warnings across both capture runs.
2. Inspect the captured PNGs directly side by side against `docs/references/shadow-tactics/st-01.jpg` through `st-25.jpg`.
3. Check house-to-person proportions, structural variation across repeated buildings, blended road-to-path and *tanada* junctions, `flora_tile_r_c` ground overgrowth and countryside groves, mip-filtered parallax relief, refractive riverbed caustics, crisp close-up foreground clarity, nocturnal Prussian-indigo roof luminance, and calm HUD legibility before accepting the change.
