# Kage no Machi direction

*Kage no Machi* is an isometric Edo post-town builder running on Flutter `3.50.0`, `flutter_scene` `0.24.3`, `flutter_scene_soloud` `0.2.0`, and macOS Metal. You shape a riverside highway post-town, or *shukuba*, from golden dusk into blue-hour night while every road, garden path, stone terrace, wall, rock outcrop, cottage, shop, shrine, and tree responds with spatial pentatonic instruments.

## Visual commitment to a fine-detail painted diorama

*Kage no Machi* commits to Style B: **Warm Sunset Gouache Dusk + Luminous Blue-Hour Night + *Urushi* & *Washi* HUD**, combining seamless hand-painted gouache textures and botanical sprays anchored in `docs/references/midjourney/` with Blender 5.3.0 hero `.glb` landmarks, `docs/references/shadow-tactics/st-01.jpg` through `st-25.jpg`, and historical Azuchi-Momoyama / early Edo village carpentry.

1. **Human-scale four-sided Edo carpentry, structural building variations, sculpted hero `.glb` landmarks, and Midjourney botanical sprays.** Dwellings scale around `1.66 m` townsfolk with `1.8 m` doorway lintels and `3.1 m` to `4.5 m` roof ridges. Sculpted Blender 5.3.0 `.glb` hero assets (`assets/models/edo_*.glb`) anchor the arched *ran-kan* vermilion bridge, upturned *sori* *torii* gate, 5-tier *Gojū-no-tō* hilltop pagoda, *Kasuga-dōrō* stone lanterns, cargo *niguruma* handcarts, and *komodaru* sake casks alongside procedural architecture. Midjourney seamless `.fstex` surface textures (`assets/textures/*.png`) and alpha-keyed painterly foliage sprites (`foliage_maple`, `foliage_willow`, `foliage_pine`, `foliage_bamboo`) bind through [`gouache_lit.fmat`](../assets/materials/gouache_lit.fmat) and [`foliage_canopy.fmat`](../assets/materials/foliage_canopy.fmat) on towering `7.8 m` to `13.5 m` trees, while `3 x 4` cardinal-quadrant mountain ridge arcs frame the valley in layered *ukiyo-e* atmospheric depth. Repeated placements cycle across four *minka* farmhouse silhouettes, four *machiya* shop silhouettes, three *chaya* teahouses, three *kura* storehouses, and four *yatai* market stalls, advancing automatically on placement or when you press `V`.
2. **Unified physical sky IBL, Style B golden-hour contrast, shadow-map *komorebi* god rays, and clustered street lanterns.** [`EdoLookDriver`](../lib/shukuba/look/edo_look_driver.dart) pairs `PhysicalSkySource`, `SkyEnvironment`, and `Skybox` with a single 4-cascade `DirectionalLight` using `DirectionalShadowFilter.pcss` across `145 m` and screen-space contact shadows. Warm golden dusk lighting (`environmentIntensity: 4.40`, `exposure: 1.00`) contrasts warm sunlit roofs and glowing *chōchin* lanterns against cool Prussian-indigo shadows (`shadowAmbientStrength` in `[0.28, 0.36]`) while transitioning smoothly into luminous Blue-Hour Night (`environmentIntensity: 1.45`, `exposure: 0.98`, zero pitch-black voids). Sixty-four-step volumetric `GodRaysPass` ray-marching (`54 m`), [`sumi_ink.frag`](../shaders/sumi_ink.frag) depth-gated mini-Kuwahara gouache brushwork with Prussian-indigo shadow glaze, organic sumi ink contours, and fine *washi* paper tooth finish the painterly look alongside [`AtmosphereBuilder`](../lib/shukuba/world/atmosphere_builder.dart).
3. **Mip-filtered parallax relief, blended road junctions, lush `4 x 4` flora chunks, and refractive caustics water.** [`gouache_lit.fmat`](../assets/materials/gouache_lit.fmat) computes analytical `fwidth` mip weights, marches a 4-step view-ray heightfield in `SampleReliefHeight`, evaluates 2-tap directional micro-horizon self-shadowing, and writes cavity darkening alongside wide bent-normal GTAO and `SamplePaintedLinear` Midjourney surface textures. [`TerrainBuilder`](../lib/shukuba/world/terrain_builder.dart) encodes longitudinal tip feathering and road-to-path junction weights into `GetUV1`, [`FloraScatterBuilder`](../lib/shukuba/world/flora_scatter_builder.dart) scatters meadow ground cover in `4 x 4` chunks through [`living_meadow.fmat`](../assets/materials/living_meadow.fmat), and [`river_water.fmat`](../assets/materials/river_water.fmat) samples `scene_color` and `scene_depth` with `GetSceneWorldPosition` for jade-teal Gerstner refraction and riverbed caustics.
4. **Sculpted multi-tier elevation, *urushi* & *washi* HUD shell, incremental entity caching, and valley-focal audio.** Terrain uses a `0.5 m` contour-aligned mesh tiled across a `4 x 4` chunk grid spanning `176 m x 176 m` with *tanada* paddies, *mitsuke* checkpoints, *ishigaki* walls, and walkable *kaidan* staircases. Startup paints the framed *Kage no Machi* **Title Menu** over a painted dusk backdrop (`assets/ui/title_backdrop.jpg`), hammered antique-brass maple crest (`assets/ui/urushi_crest.png`), falling *momiji*/*ichō* leaves, and *kinpaku* gold flecks ([`shukuba_shell_overlays.dart`](../lib/shukuba/view/shukuba_shell_overlays.dart)) while audio prewarm runs in a background isolate. [`ShukubaHud`](../lib/shukuba/view/shukuba_hud.dart) keeps over ninety percent of the viewport open with deep *urushi* lacquer & hammered-brass header pills (`44 px`), `32 px` lacquered dock tabs, and `42 px` cream *washi* tool cards showcasing Midjourney woodblock architectural artwork (`assets/ui/icon_*.png`) that glow cinnabar-amber when selected. [`EdoAudioDriver`](../lib/shukuba/audio/edo_audio_driver.dart) mounts `SoloudAudioEngine` with valley-focal spatial pentatonic audio, while [`EdoWorldDriver`](../lib/shukuba/world/edo_world_driver.dart) caches unchanged structure `Node`s and mounts hero `.glb` landmarks cleanly.

## Parked systems for watchtowers and stealth intrigue

Watchtowers, Magistrate's Eye sightcone decals, and nighttime smuggling mechanics remain parked while work focuses on tactile Edo city building and painted diorama craft. Low-level `DecalNode` shader support stays in the codebase for future use, while watchtowers and vision-cone overlays remain out of the active HUD, starter ward, and player toolset.

## Visual evaluation loop

Every rendering, shader, or geometry change follows a closed Metal screenshot loop:

1. Capture settled `1440x778` frames across all six valley presets plus `horizon_sun_canopy` and `horizon_hillside_tilt` with [`ShukubaFrameHarness`](../lib/shukuba/verify/frame_harness.dart), plus 4-corner building turntables, ten close-up geometry-seam cameras, and live UI screenshots via `SHUKUBA_CAPTURE_ATLAS=true`. On time-of-day transitions, the harness pumps `18` frames so `SkyEnvironment` finishes its 16-step time-sliced cubemap, prefilter, and spherical-harmonics bake before reading pixels, while unchanged time-of-day shots pump `2` frames. Confirm zero coplanar overlaps, zero perturbed depth conflicts, and zero engine punctual-light or near-plane warnings across both capture runs.
2. Inspect the captured PNGs directly side by side against `docs/references/shadow-tactics/st-01.jpg` through `st-25.jpg`.
3. Check house-to-person proportions, structural variation across repeated buildings, blended road-to-path and *tanada* junctions, `flora_tile_r_c` ground overgrowth and countryside groves, mip-filtered parallax relief, refractive riverbed caustics, crisp close-up foreground clarity, nocturnal Prussian-indigo roof luminance, and calm HUD legibility before accepting the change.

Work in this repository follows strict model ownership:

- **Carbon** owns all 3D geometry, `.fmat` and post-processing shaders, visual evaluation against the reference corpus, UX design, deep research, and orchestration.
- **Argon** assists with bounded systems plumbing, unit tests, and static or runtime verification, always reviewed and visually verified by Carbon.
