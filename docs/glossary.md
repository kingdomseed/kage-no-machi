# Glossary

This reference defines the Edo architectural, botanical, topographic, atmospheric, musical, UI, and `flutter_scene` rendering terms used across *Kage no Machi* and maps each term to plain English and its codebase location.

## Edo architecture, topography, flora, and street life

| Term | Plain-English meaning | Visual and gameplay role in *Kage no Machi* | Codebase mapping |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *Shukuba* | Post-town along an Edo highway | The riverside settlement ward that you build and illuminate | `ShukubaGraph` in `lib/shukuba/model/shukuba_types.dart` |
| *Kaidō* | Edo overland highway and town road | Catmull-Rom packed-earth thoroughfare with parallel cartwheel ruts, pebbles, damp puddles, feathered endpoints, and 3D *fuchi-ishi* stone curbs | `RoadStyle.kaidoRoad`, **Road** in `ShukubaHud`, `RoadSpline`, and `TerrainBuilder.buildRoadNode` |
| *Roji* & *Tobi-ishi* | Winding garden path and stepping stones | Connected boulder-free raked-gravel walkway studded with proud 3D slate stepping stones, pebble edging, and smooth junction cross-blending into parent roads | `RoadStyle.gardenPath`, **Garden Path** in `ShukubaHud`, and `TerrainBuilder.buildRoadNode` |
| *Iwa* & *Iwakura* | Layered rock outcrop and boulder triad | Grounded `1.4 m` to `2.6 m` fractured slate-and-ochre rock shelves, angular ledges, and mossy *sanson-seki* garden stones cleared away from road and path corridors | `StructureKind.rockOutcrop`, **Rock Outcrop**, `gouache_lit.fmat`, and `EdoKitBuilder.buildRockOutcropNode` |
| *Ishigaki* & *Sangi-zumi* | Fitted dry-stone retaining wall and corner quoins | Battered polygonal castle-stone wall retaining a raised earth plateau, crowned with individual capstones and interlocking corner quoins | `TerraceCourse`, **Stone Terrace**, `ShukubaSurfaceRole.ishigakiStone`, and `TerrainBuilder.buildTerraceNode` |
| *Kaidan* & *Shikishi* | Stepped stone or timber staircase and threshold paving | Walkable cut-stone and log-riser steps with flush cut-stone landing aprons wherever a road or path crosses a terrace rise | `TerrainBuilder.emitKaidanStaircase`, `buildTerraceNode`, and `sampleGroundHeight` |
| *Shigarami* | Riverside timber piling and log revetment | Driven wooden bank stakes and horizontal retaining logs stabilizing river bends and bridge approaches | `TerrainBuilder.buildGorgeDressingNode` in `lib/shukuba/world/terrain_builder.dart` |
| *Tanada*, *Aze*, & *Hi-no-kuchi* | Terraced hillside rice paddies, earthen bunds, and wooden spillways | Six contour-graded crescent paddy shelves with `0.44 m` to `0.54 m` steps on the eastern amphitheater with mossy bunds, wooden irrigation troughs, calm water, and cutout *nae* shoots | `TerrainBuilder.sampleTanadaGradedElevation` and `buildTanadaPaddiesNode` |
| *Mitsuke* & *Masugata* | Highway checkpoint gate and stone bastion pocket | East and West *kaidō* approach gates with dressed corner quoins, gateway threshold curbs, *kaidan* steps, and tiled timber gatehouses | `TerrainBuilder.buildMitsukeCheckpointsNode` in `lib/shukuba/world/terrain_builder.dart` |
| *Machiya*, *Udatsu*, & *Nurigome* | Merchant townhouse, wing fire-walls, and heavy plaster shop | Four `3.8 m x 4.8 m` townhouse silhouettes spanning *kawara* shops, rooftop *hoshidai* artisan lofts, *udatsu* dyer houses, and *nurigome* apothecaries | `StructureKind.machiya`, `PlacedStructure.variant`, and `EdoKitBuilder.buildMachiyaNode` |
| *Minka*, *Magariya*, & *Kayabuki* | Vernacular farmhouse, L-plan cottage, and golden reed thatch | Four `3.6 m x 4.2 m` rural silhouettes spanning hipped *kayabuki* thatch, L-wing *magariya* with *hasaki* rice racks, stone-shingle workshops, and *hisashi* homesteads | `StructureKind.minka`, `PlacedStructure.variant`, and `EdoKitBuilder.buildMinkaNode` |
| *Chaya* & *Kakejaya* | Roadside teahouse, open pavilion, and river lantern inn | Three `4.4 m x 4.4 m` hospitality silhouettes spanning garden *irimoya* teahouses, thatched *kakejaya* rest booths, and two-story *kōran* balcony inns | `StructureKind.chaya`, `PlacedStructure.variant`, and `EdoKitBuilder.buildChayaNode` |
| *Kura* & *Ita-gura* | Earthen-plaster vault and interlocking cedar-plank granary | Three `3.4 m x 4.0 m` storehouse silhouettes spanning white *namako* vaults, black *kuro-shikkui* vaults, and elevated *azekura* cedar granaries | `StructureKind.kura`, `PlacedStructure.variant`, and `EdoKitBuilder.buildKuraNode` |
| *Yatai* | Open street stall and wheeled *soba* cart | Four `2.2 m x 1.9 m` stall silhouettes spanning striped produce booths, wheeled noodle carts, *wagasa* rice stands, and *sakaya* cask booths | `StructureKind.marketStall`, `PlacedStructure.variant`, and `EdoKitBuilder.buildMarketStallNode` |
| *Kutsunugi-ishi* | Cut-stone doorway threshold step | Dressed granite step block set proud of house foundation plinths below front and *doma* entrances | `EdoKitBuilder` house builders and `ShukubaSurfaceRole.ishigakiStone` |
| *Shōrō* & *Bonshō* | Raised timber bell pavilion and bronze temple bell | Stepped `3.2 m x 3.2 m` timber belfry deck with balustrades, concave *irimoya* roof, and suspended bronze bell | `StructureKind.shoroBell`, **Bell Pavilion**, and `EdoKitBuilder.buildShoroBellNode` |
| *Karesansui* | Raked dry gravel garden | Bordered `4.2 m x 3.6 m` white-gravel courtyard with concentric raked ripples, mossy *sanson-seki* triad, azaleas, and carved *tōrō* lantern | `StructureKind.karesansuiGarden`, **Zen Gravel Court**, and `EdoKitBuilder.buildKaresansuiGardenNode` |
| *Ido* | Roofed stone courtyard well | Circular `1.8 m x 1.8 m` masonry well curb with timber posts, shingle gable roof, pulley wheel, and wooden water buckets | `StructureKind.stoneWell`, **Courtyard Well**, and `EdoKitBuilder.buildStoneWellNode` |
| *Hei* | Tiled courtyard wall | Continuous white-plaster and timber compound wall on a mossy stone plinth capped by a pitched *kawara* tile roof | `CourtyardWall`, **Courtyard Wall**, and `TerrainBuilder.buildWallNode` |
| *Torii* & lantern gate | Vermilion highway archway | Framed `4.0 m` wide cinnabar-lacquer gateway with *kasagi* top lintel and hanging lanterns that straddles lanes automatically when `straddlesRoad` is true | `StructureKind.toriiGate`, **Lantern Gate**, and `EdoKitBuilder.buildToriiGateNode` |
| *Shitami-ita*, *Tobukuro*, & *Inuyarai* | Horizontal cedar siding, shutter box, and curved mud-guard | Four-sided exterior carpentry details clothing side and rear house walls and recessed shop fronts | `EdoKitBuilder` house builders and `ShukubaSurfaceRole.charredTimber` |
| *Hoshidai* & *Kemuridashi* | Rooftop wooden deck and ridge smoke monitor | Timber laundry or fire-bucket roof platform with exterior ladder and raised kitchen smoke vent | `EdoKitBuilder.buildMachiyaNode` and `EdoKitBuilder.buildMinkaNode` |
| *Irimoya*, *Kawara*, & *Onigawara* | Hip-and-gable roof, clay tiles, and ridge finials | Concave-eaved gable-over-hip roofs, hand-dipped variegated clay tile courses with `style_id = 1.0`, projecting *taruki* rafters, and sculpted ridge caps | `ShukubaSurfaceRole.kawaraTile`, `EdoKitBuilder.addIrimoyaRoof`, and `addGabledRoof` |
| *Fukinagashi*, *Noren*, & *Wagasa* | Festival cloth streamers, doorway curtain, and red oiled-paper umbrella | Multi-color fluttering cloth garlands strung along catenary cords, split shop curtains, and roadside umbrellas | `LanternSpan.hasFestivalStreamers`, `EdoKitBuilder.buildLanternSpanNode`, and `addWagasaUmbrella` |
| *Komodaru* & *Daihachi-guruma* | Wrapped straw sake casks and two-wheeled wooden handcart | Stacked barrel racks, spoked cargo carts, grain sacks, wooden water tubs, and muddy-lane plank duckboards | `EdoKitBuilder` threshold prop helpers |
| *Chōchin*, *Shōji*, & *Tōrō* | Hanging paper lantern, paper screen, and carved stone lantern | Faceted amber street lanterns clustered into at most `12` warm `PointLight` pools, glowing interior window screens, and mossy stone pedestal lanterns | `LanternSpan`, `EdoLookDriver.clusterLanternOrigins`, and `assets/materials/shoji_lantern.fmat` |
| *Haori*, *Tamoto*, & *Oi* | Layered kimono jacket, hanging sleeve pouches, and peddler pack | Stylized `1.66 m` Edo townsfolk silhouettes with draped sleeves, *obi* sashes, *sugegasa* hats, *oi* packs, and pole lanterns | `EdoKitBuilder.buildStreetAgentNode` in `lib/shukuba/world/edo_kit_builder.dart` |
| *Momiji*, *Kuromatsu*, *Sugi*, *Yanagi*, & *Take* | Autumn maple, black pine, tall cedar, weeping willow, and jointed bamboo | Towering `7.8 m` to `13.5 m` per-tree DNA builds across town courtyards and 34 outer countryside groves in `starter_ward.dart` | `EdoKitBuilder` tree builders, `buildStarterShukubaGraph`, and `assets/materials/foliage_canopy.fmat` |
| *Shita-kusa*, *Susuki*, *Nogiku*, *Higanbana*, & *Ashi* | Clover, fern, and moss overgrowth, pampas plumes, wild daisies, spider lilies, and bank reeds | Ground-hugging 9-frond clover, fern, and moss rosettes plus grass tufts, wildflowers, wall drapes, and river reeds partitioned across sixteen `flora_tile_r_c` chunks | `FloraScatterBuilder`, `FloraChunkSummary`, and `assets/materials/living_meadow.fmat` |
| *Komorebi*, *Kawagiri*, & *Hotaru* | Canopy sunbeams, rising river-gorge mist, and evening fireflies | Cascade-bounded volumetric god rays filtering through tree canopies from the skybox sun, depth-softened curl-noise mist ribbons, chimney steam, dusk pollen, and night fireflies | `AtmosphereBuilder`, `EdoLookDriver`, `assets/materials/atmosphere_mist.fmat`, and `assets/materials/airborne_motes.fmat` |
| *Yagura* | Parked timber watchtower | Elevated lookout tower reserved for future watch and intrigue mechanics | Retained in `StructureKind.yaguraTower` for compatibility and omitted from starter ward and HUD |

## Musical and audio terms

| Term | Plain-English meaning | Role in *Kage no Machi* | Codebase mapping |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *Yo* scale | Bright daytime Japanese pentatonic scale `D E G A B` | Quantizes placement notes when `timeOfDay < 0.55` | `PentatonicScale.yo` in `lib/shukuba/model/shukuba_types.dart` |
| *In-sen* scale | Nocturnal Japanese pentatonic scale `D Eb G A C` | Quantizes placement notes when `timeOfDay >= 0.55` | `PentatonicScale.inSen` in `lib/shukuba/model/shukuba_types.dart` |
| *Koto* | Plucked 13-string zither | Plays `D4`-calibrated plucked string notes when placing townhouses, farmhouses, teahouses, lantern gates, and maples | `assets/audio/inst_koto.ogg`, `EdoInstrument.koto`, and `EdoWavBank` fallback in `lib/shukuba/audio/edo_wav_synth.dart` |
| *Shakuhachi* | End-blown bamboo flute | Plays `D4`-calibrated breathy flute notes when planting bamboo groves, weeping willows, and zen gravel courts | `assets/audio/inst_shakuhachi.ogg`, `EdoInstrument.shakuhachi`, and `EdoWavBank` fallback in `lib/shukuba/audio/edo_wav_synth.dart` |
| *Hyōshigi* | Crisp wooden clappers | Plays rhythmic wood-block clicks for market stalls, courtyard wells, roads, garden paths, courtyard walls, demolish, and undo | `assets/audio/inst_hyoshigi.ogg`, `EdoInstrument.hyoshigi`, and `EdoWavBank` fallback in `lib/shukuba/audio/edo_wav_synth.dart` |
| *Bonshō* | Deep bronze temple bell | Plays resonant low bell tones when raising bell pavilions, stone terraces, *kura* storehouses, black pines, and rock outcrops | `assets/audio/inst_bonsho.ogg`, `EdoInstrument.bonsho`, and `EdoWavBank` fallback in `lib/shukuba/audio/edo_wav_synth.dart` |
| `music_shukuba.ogg` & `_musicBus` | Licensed traditional Japanese background music | Loops non-positionally on the **Title Menu** and inside the valley on `_musicBus` | `assets/audio/music_shukuba.ogg`, `EdoAudioDriver.startMenuMusic`, and `EdoAudioDriver.attach` |
| `ambient_river.ogg`, `ambient_wind.ogg`, & `ambient_crickets.ogg` | Gentle natural field loops | Spatializes calm brook, foliage wind, and evening cricket ambience across dusk and night with `EdoWavBank` fallback | `assets/audio/ambient_*.ogg`, `AmbientLoopKind`, and `TimeOfDayRig` in `lib/shukuba/model/shukuba_types.dart` |
| `edo_audio_listener` | Valley-focal isometric audio listener | Anchors `AudioListener` above `PerspectiveCamera.target` at zoom-scaled ear height with horizontal camera facing and zero Doppler | `EdoAudioDriver.computeListenerPose` in `lib/shukuba/audio/edo_audio_driver.dart` |

## UI shell, rendering, and engine terms

| Term | Plain-English meaning | Role in *Kage no Machi* | Codebase mapping |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *Urushi* & *Washi* UI shell | Smoked-lacquer timber frames, warm paper borders, and cinnabar seals | Cohesive *Kage no Machi* **Title Menu** with falling *momiji* maple and *ichō* ginkgo leaves over *kinpaku* gold flecks, **Loading Curtain**, `Esc` **Pause Menu**, `F3` telemetry pill, collapsible **Views** bar, and single-drawer builder dock | `ShukubaShellMode`, `AutumnLeavesPainter`, `KinpakuPainter`, `lib/shukuba/view/shukuba_shell_overlays.dart`, and `ShukubaHud` |
| `ShukubaHud` uniform button grid | Shortcut-free single-line HUD controls | Renders uniform `32 px` single-line HUD buttons with `15 px` `InkBrushIcon`s and hover-tooltip shortcuts alongside `42 px` drawer cards | `_HudButton`, `_ToolCardButton`, and `InkBrushIcon` in `lib/shukuba/view/shukuba_hud.dart` |
| `BootReport` & `FrameTelemetrySnapshot` | Stage-timed startup log and live UI and raster frame monitor | Records off-isolate audio synthesis, shader load, staged world build, GPU warmup, and rolling UI and raster frame times | `lib/shukuba/view/shukuba_boot_models.dart` and `ShukubaPage` |
| `PhysicalSkySource` & `SkyEnvironment` | Preetham Rayleigh-Mie sky dome and time-sliced IBL baker | Bakes cubemap, prefiltered specular mips, and spherical-harmonics irradiance locked to `TimeOfDayRig.celestialSkyDirection` so skybox sun/moon, IBL, shadows, and 64-step `GodRaysPass` shafts align across dusk and night | `EdoLookDriver._setupLightingAndSky`, `_applySkyForRig`, and `syncFrame` |
| `DirectionalShadowFilter.pcss` | Percentage-closer soft shadows with contact hardening | Renders 4-cascade soft sun and moon shadows across `145 m` with `0.052 rad` source radius and `0.45 m` screen-space contact shadows | `EdoLookDriver._ensureLightPool` in `lib/shukuba/look/edo_look_driver.dart` |
| `clusterLanternOrigins` | Greedy `5.5 m` spatial lantern clustering | Merges street and eave lantern anchors into at most `12` weighted `PointLight` instances with `8.2 m` range so `flutter_scene` stays within its 16-light budget | `EdoLookDriver.clusterLanternOrigins` in `lib/shukuba/look/edo_look_driver.dart` |
| `SampleReliefHeight` & `fwidth` mip-filtering | Analytical derivative anti-aliasing and 4-step parallax relief | Filters procedural noise by pixel footprint, marches a 4-step view-ray heightfield, applies 2-tap micro-horizon self-shadowing, and drives `material.occlusion` | `assets/materials/gouache_lit.fmat` |
| `GetUV1` lane cross-blending | Secondary UV channel encoding tip feathering and road-to-path mix | Feathers road endpoints into natural ground and cross-blends `EvaluateGardenPath` into `EvaluateKaidoRoad` across shared junctions | `TerrainBuilder.buildRoadNode` and `assets/materials/gouache_lit.fmat` |
| `RiverWater` Beer-Lambert caustics | Depth-refracted water transmission and riverbed light webs | Samples `scene_color` and `scene_depth` with `GetSceneWorldPosition` to refract riverbed stones, attenuate RGB by water thickness, and project dual cellular caustics | `assets/materials/river_water.fmat` |
| `flora_tile_r_c` | `4 x 4` spatial ground-flora chunk nodes | Splits instanced meadow tufts, 9-frond overgrowth, reeds, wall drapes, and bank cobbles into sixteen `44 m x 44 m` tiles for dirty-rect rebuilds and frustum culling | `FloraScatterBuilder`, `FloraChunkSummary`, and `EdoWorldDriver` |
| `shadowStatic` | Cached static directional shadow-map caster flag | Marks non-displaced terrain, masonry, bridge, and static building meshes so `flutter_scene` reuses cached shadow tiles | `Node.shadowStatic` in `TerrainBuilder`, `EdoKitBuilder`, and `EdoWorldDriver` |
| `.fmat` | `flutter_scene` custom material source file | Compiled offline by `hook/build.dart` into SPIR-V and loaded via `loadFmatMaterial` | `assets/materials/*.fmat` and `hook/build.dart` |
| `alpha_to_coverage` | MSAA sub-pixel cutout coverage and shadow discard | Gives leaf sprays and habitat-pocket grass tufts soft cutout silhouettes, exact prepass normals, and perforated PCSS shadows | `assets/materials/foliage_canopy.fmat` and `assets/materials/living_meadow.fmat` |
| `InstancedMesh` | Single-draw GPU hardware instancing | Draws `flora_tile_r_c` overgrowth, grass tufts, *susuki* plumes, wildflowers, reeds, wall drapes, lantern halos, and airborne motes in batched GPU draws | `FloraScatterBuilder`, `AtmosphereBuilder`, and `assets/materials/living_meadow.fmat` |
| `dewFactor` | Time-of-day surface moisture curve | Modulates evening roughness on roof tiles, cobbles, dirt hollows, and bridge planks to produce damp sky reflections | `TimeOfDayRig.dewFactor` in `lib/shukuba/model/shukuba_types.dart` and `assets/materials/gouache_lit.fmat` |
| `SumiInkPass` | Post-processing ink crease pass | Reads linear depth, octahedral normals, and surface roughness at `RenderStage.afterToneMapping` to draw brush-ink contours | `lib/shukuba/look/sumi_ink_pass.dart` and `shaders/sumi_ink.frag` |
| `depthLayer` | Polygon-offset priority tier from `-8` to `8` | Separates terrain, structures, stone curbs, and flora at `0`, road, garden, and water overlays at `1`, stepping stones and build previews at `2`, and mist and motes at `3` through `4` | `ShukubaSurfaceRole.depthLayer` in `lib/shukuba/model/shukuba_types.dart` |
| `ShukubaFrameHarness` & `SHUKUBA_CAPTURE_ATLAS` | Automated Metal capture, seam atlas, and 4-angle turntable runner | Pumps `18` frames on time-of-day sky-bake transitions and `2` frames otherwise, checks `findCoplanarOverlaps` and `probeDepthConflicts`, and saves valley, seam, 22-specimen turntable, and UI PNGs | `lib/shukuba/verify/frame_harness.dart` and `lib/shukuba/view/shukuba_page.dart` |
