# Glossary

This reference defines the Edo architectural, botanical, topographic, atmospheric, musical, UI, and `flutter_scene` rendering terms used across *Kage no Machi* and maps each term to plain English and its codebase location.

## Edo architecture, topography, flora, and street life

| Term | Plain-English meaning | Visual and gameplay role in *Kage no Machi* | Codebase mapping |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *Shukuba* | Post-town along an Edo highway | The riverside settlement ward that you build and illuminate | `ShukubaGraph` in `lib/shukuba/model/shukuba_types.dart` |
| *Kaidō* | Edo overland highway and town road | Smooth Catmull-Rom packed-earth thoroughfare with parallel cartwheel ruts, pebbles, damp puddles, and 3D *fuchi-ishi* stone curbs | `RoadStyle.kaidoRoad` (`Road` in `ShukubaHud`), `RoadSpline`, and `TerrainBuilder.buildRoadNode` |
| *Roji* & *Tobi-ishi* | Winding garden path and stepping stones | Narrow curved raked-gravel walkway studded with proud 3D slate stepping stones and pebble edging | `RoadStyle.gardenPath` (`Garden Path` in `ShukubaHud`) and `TerrainBuilder.buildRoadNode` |
| *Iwa* / *Iwakura* | Layered rock outcrop and boulder triad | Grounded `1.4 m` to `2.6 m` fractured slate-and-ochre rock shelves, angular ledges, and mossy *sanson-seki* garden stones | `StructureKind.rockOutcrop` (`Rock Outcrop`), `gouache_lit.fmat`, and `EdoKitBuilder.buildRockOutcropNode` |
| *Ishigaki* & *Sangi-zumi* | Fitted dry-stone retaining wall and corner quoins | Battered polygonal castle-stone wall retaining a raised earth plateau, crowned with individual capstones and interlocking corner quoins | `TerraceCourse` (`Stone Terrace`), `ShukubaSurfaceRole.ishigakiStone`, and `TerrainBuilder.buildTerraceNode` |
| *Kaidan* & *Shikishi* | Stepped stone or timber staircase and threshold paving | Walkable cut-stone and log-riser steps with flush cut-stone landing aprons wherever a road or path crosses a terrace rise | `TerrainBuilder.emitKaidanStaircase`, `buildTerraceNode`, and `sampleGroundHeight` |
| *Shigarami* | Riverside timber piling and log revetment | Driven wooden bank stakes and horizontal retaining logs stabilizing river bends and bridge approaches | `TerrainBuilder.buildGorgeDressingNode` in `lib/shukuba/world/terrain_builder.dart` |
| *Tanada*, *Aze*, & *Hi-no-kuchi* | Terraced hillside rice paddies, earthen bunds, and wooden spillways | Six contour-graded crescent paddy shelves (`0.44 m` to `0.54 m` steps) on the eastern amphitheater with mossy bunds, wooden irrigation troughs, calm water, and cutout *nae* shoots | `TerrainBuilder.sampleTanadaGradedElevation` and `buildTanadaPaddiesNode` |
| *Mitsuke* & *Masugata* | Highway checkpoint gate and stone bastion pocket | East and West *kaidō* approach gates with dressed corner quoins, gateway threshold curbs, *kaidan* steps, and tiled timber gatehouses | `TerrainBuilder.buildMitsukeCheckpointsNode` in `lib/shukuba/world/terrain_builder.dart` |
| *Machiya*, *Udatsu*, & *Nurigome* | Merchant townhouse, wing fire-walls, and heavy plaster shop | Four townhouse silhouettes (`3.8 m x 4.8 m`) spanning *kawara* shops, rooftop *hoshidai* artisan lofts, *udatsu* dyer houses, and *nurigome* apothecaries | `StructureKind.machiya`, `PlacedStructure.variant`, and `EdoKitBuilder.buildMachiyaNode` |
| *Minka*, *Magariya*, & *Kayabuki* | Vernacular farmhouse, L-plan cottage, and golden reed thatch | Four rural silhouettes (`3.6 m x 4.2 m`) spanning hipped *kayabuki* thatch, L-wing *magariya* with *hasaki* rice racks, stone-shingle workshops, and *hisashi* homesteads | `StructureKind.minka`, `PlacedStructure.variant`, and `EdoKitBuilder.buildMinkaNode` |
| *Chaya* & *Kakejaya* | Roadside teahouse, open pavilion, and river lantern inn | Three hospitality silhouettes (`4.4 m x 4.4 m`) spanning garden *irimoya* teahouses, thatched *kakejaya* rest booths, and two-story *kōran* balcony inns | `StructureKind.chaya`, `PlacedStructure.variant`, and `EdoKitBuilder.buildChayaNode` |
| *Kura* & *Ita-gura* | Earthen-plaster vault and interlocking cedar-plank granary | Three storehouse silhouettes (`3.4 m x 4.0 m`) spanning white *namako* vaults, black *kuro-shikkui* vaults, and elevated *azekura* cedar granaries | `StructureKind.kura`, `PlacedStructure.variant`, and `EdoKitBuilder.buildKuraNode` |
| *Yatai* | Open street stall and wheeled *soba* cart | Four stall silhouettes (`2.2 m x 1.9 m`) spanning striped produce booths, wheeled noodle carts, *wagasa* rice stands, and *sakaya* cask booths | `StructureKind.marketStall`, `PlacedStructure.variant`, and `EdoKitBuilder.buildMarketStallNode` |
| *Kutsunugi-ishi* | Cut-stone doorway threshold step | Dressed granite step block set proud of house foundation plinths below front and *doma* entrances | `EdoKitBuilder` house builders (`ShukubaSurfaceRole.ishigakiStone`) |
| *Shōrō* & *Bonshō* | Raised timber bell pavilion and bronze temple bell | Stepped timber belfry deck (`3.2 m x 3.2 m`) with balustrades, concave *irimoya* roof, and suspended bronze bell | `StructureKind.shoroBell` (`Bell Pavilion`) and `EdoKitBuilder.buildShoroBellNode` |
| *Karesansui* | Raked dry-landscape gravel garden | Bordered white-gravel courtyard (`4.2 m x 3.6 m`) with concentric raked ripples, mossy *sanson-seki* triad, azaleas, and carved *tōrō* lantern | `StructureKind.karesansuiGarden` (`Zen Gravel Court`) and `EdoKitBuilder.buildKaresansuiGardenNode` |
| *Ido* | Roofed stone courtyard well | Circular masonry well curb (`1.8 m x 1.8 m`) with timber posts, shingle gable roof, pulley wheel, and wooden water buckets | `StructureKind.stoneWell` (`Courtyard Well`) and `EdoKitBuilder.buildStoneWellNode` |
| *Hei* | Tiled courtyard wall | Continuous white-plaster and timber compound wall on a mossy stone plinth capped by a pitched *kawara* tile roof | `CourtyardWall` (`Courtyard Wall`) and `TerrainBuilder.buildWallNode` |
| *Torii* / Lantern gate | Vermilion highway archway | Framed cinnabar-lacquer gateway (`4.0 m` wide) with *kasagi* top lintel and hanging lanterns that straddles lanes automatically | `StructureKind.toriiGate` (`Lantern Gate`, `straddlesRoad: true`) and `EdoKitBuilder.buildToriiGateNode` |
| *Shitami-ita*, *Tobukuro*, & *Inuyarai* | Horizontal cedar siding, shutter box, and curved mud-guard | Four-sided exterior carpentry details clothing side and rear house walls and recessed shop fronts | `EdoKitBuilder` house builders (`ShukubaSurfaceRole.charredTimber`) |
| *Hoshidai* & *Kemuridashi* | Rooftop wooden deck and ridge smoke monitor | Timber laundry or fire-bucket roof platform with exterior ladder and raised kitchen smoke vent | `EdoKitBuilder.buildMachiyaNode` and `EdoKitBuilder.buildMinkaNode` |
| *Irimoya*, *Kawara*, & *Onigawara* | Hip-and-gable roof, clay tiles, and ridge finials | Concave-eaved gable-over-hip roofs, hand-dipped variegated clay tile courses, projecting *taruki* rafters, and sculpted ridge caps | `ShukubaSurfaceRole.kawaraTile` (`style_id = 1.0`), `EdoKitBuilder.addIrimoyaRoof`, and `addGabledRoof` |
| *Fukinagashi*, *Noren*, & *Wagasa* | Festival cloth streamers, doorway curtain, and red oiled-paper umbrella | Multi-color fluttering cloth garlands strung along catenary cords, split shop curtains, and roadside umbrellas | `LanternSpan.hasFestivalStreamers`, `EdoKitBuilder.buildLanternSpanNode`, and `addWagasaUmbrella` |
| *Komodaru* & *Daihachi-guruma* | Wrapped straw sake casks and two-wheeled wooden handcart | Stacked barrel racks, spoked cargo carts, grain sacks, wooden water tubs, and muddy-lane plank duckboards | `EdoKitBuilder` threshold prop helpers |
| *Chōchin*, *Shōji*, & *Tōrō* | Hanging paper lantern, paper screen, and carved stone lantern | Faceted amber street lanterns on catenary cords, glowing interior window screens, and mossy stone pedestal lanterns | `LanternSpan`, `EdoKitBuilder.addStoneToro`, and `assets/materials/shoji_lantern.fmat` |
| *Haori*, *Tamoto*, & *Oi* | Layered kimono jacket, hanging sleeve pouches, and peddler pack | Stylized `1.66 m` Edo townsfolk silhouettes with draped sleeves, *obi* sashes, *sugegasa* hats, *oi* packs, and pole lanterns | `EdoKitBuilder.buildStreetAgentNode` in `lib/shukuba/world/edo_kit_builder.dart` |
| *Momiji*, *Kuromatsu*, *Sugi*, *Yanagi*, & *Take* | Autumn maple, black pine, tall cedar, weeping willow, and jointed bamboo | Towering `7.8 m` to `13.5 m` per-tree DNA botanical builds with bare jointed lower bamboo culms, cloud-pruned pine shelves, and conical cedars | `EdoKitBuilder` tree builders and `assets/materials/foliage_canopy.fmat` |
| *Susuki*, *Nogiku*, *Higanbana*, & *Ashi* | Pampas plumes, wild daisies, red spider lilies, and bank reeds | Restrained habitat-pocket ground flora hugging stone walls, boulders, tree skirts, and river margins with calm ambient sway | `FloraScatterBuilder`, `ShukubaSurfaceRole.livingMeadow`, and `assets/materials/living_meadow.fmat` |
| *Kawagiri* & *Hotaru* | Rising river-gorge mist and evening fireflies | Depth-softened curl-noise mist ribbons above the stream, dusk pollen and falling leaves, and glowing night fireflies | `TimeOfDayRig` curves, `assets/materials/atmosphere_mist.fmat`, and `assets/materials/airborne_motes.fmat` |
| *Yagura* | Timber watchtower (parked) | Elevated lookout tower reserved for future watch and intrigue mechanics | Retained in `StructureKind.yaguraTower` for compatibility and omitted from starter ward and HUD |

## Musical and audio terms

| Term | Plain-English meaning | Role in *Kage no Machi* | Codebase mapping |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *Yo* scale | Bright daytime Japanese pentatonic scale (`D E G A B`) | Quantizes placement notes when `timeOfDay < 0.55` | `PentatonicScale.yo` in `lib/shukuba/model/shukuba_types.dart` |
| *In-sen* scale | Nocturnal Japanese pentatonic scale (`D Eb G A C`) | Quantizes placement notes when `timeOfDay >= 0.55` | `PentatonicScale.inSen` in `lib/shukuba/model/shukuba_types.dart` |
| *Koto* | Plucked 13-string zither | Plays plucked string notes when placing townhouses, farmhouses, teahouses, lantern gates, and maples | `EdoInstrument.koto` in `lib/shukuba/audio/edo_wav_synth.dart` |
| *Shakuhachi* | End-blown bamboo flute | Plays breathy flute notes when planting bamboo groves, weeping willows, and zen gravel courts | `EdoInstrument.shakuhachi` in `lib/shukuba/audio/edo_wav_synth.dart` |
| *Hyōshigi* | Crisp wooden clappers | Plays rhythmic wood-block clicks for market stalls, courtyard wells, roads, garden paths, courtyard walls, demolish, and undo | `EdoInstrument.hyoshigi` in `lib/shukuba/audio/edo_wav_synth.dart` |
| *Bonshō* | Deep bronze temple bell | Plays resonant low bell tones when raising bell pavilions, stone terraces, *kura* storehouses, black pines, and rock outcrops | `EdoInstrument.bonsho` in `lib/shukuba/audio/edo_wav_synth.dart` |

## UI shell, rendering, and engine terms

| Term | Plain-English meaning | Role in *Kage no Machi* | Codebase mapping |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *Urushi* & *Washi* UI shell | Smoked-lacquer timber frames, warm paper borders, and cinnabar seals | Cohesive *Kage no Machi* Title Menu with live audio settings, Phased Loading Curtain, `Esc` Pause Menu, `F3` Telemetry pill, collapsible `Views` bar, and calm single-drawer builder dock | `ShukubaShellMode`, `lib/shukuba/view/shukuba_shell_overlays.dart`, and `ShukubaHud` |
| `BootReport` & `FrameTelemetrySnapshot` | Stage-timed startup log and live UI/raster frame monitor | Records off-isolate audio synthesis, shader load, staged world build, GPU warmup, and rolling UI/raster frame times | `lib/shukuba/view/shukuba_boot_models.dart` and `ShukubaPage` |
| `shadowStatic` | Cached static directional shadow-map caster flag | Marks non-displaced terrain, masonry, bridge, and static building meshes so `flutter_scene` reuses cached shadow tiles | `Node.shadowStatic` in `TerrainBuilder`, `EdoKitBuilder`, and `EdoWorldDriver` |
| `.fmat` | `flutter_scene` custom material source file | Compiled offline by `hook/build.dart` into SPIR-V and loaded via `loadFmatMaterial` | `assets/materials/*.fmat` and `hook/build.dart` |
| `alpha_to_coverage` | MSAA sub-pixel cutout coverage and shadow discard | Gives leaf sprays and habitat-pocket grass tufts soft cutout silhouettes, exact prepass normals, and perforated PCSS shadows | `assets/materials/foliage_canopy.fmat` and `assets/materials/living_meadow.fmat` |
| `InstancedMesh` | Single-draw GPU hardware instancing | Draws habitat-pocket grass tufts, *susuki* plumes, wildflowers, reeds, wall drapes, and airborne motes in batched GPU draws | `FloraScatterBuilder`, `AtmosphereBuilder`, and `assets/materials/living_meadow.fmat` |
| `dewFactor` | Time-of-day surface moisture curve | Modulates evening roughness on roof tiles, cobbles, dirt hollows, and bridge planks to produce damp sky reflections | `TimeOfDayRig.dewFactor` in `lib/shukuba/model/shukuba_types.dart` and `assets/materials/gouache_lit.fmat` |
| `SumiInkPass` | Post-processing ink crease pass | Reads linear depth, octahedral normals, and surface roughness at `RenderStage.afterToneMapping` to draw brush-ink contours | `lib/shukuba/look/sumi_ink_pass.dart` and `shaders/sumi_ink.frag` |
| `depthLayer` | Polygon-offset priority tier (`-8` to `8`) | Separates terrain, structures, stone curbs, and flora (`0`), road, garden, and water overlays (`1`), stepping stones and build previews (`2`), and mist/motes (`3`..`4`) | `ShukubaSurfaceRole.depthLayer` in `lib/shukuba/model/shukuba_types.dart` |
| `ShukubaFrameHarness` & `SHUKUBA_CAPTURE_ATLAS` | Automated Metal capture, seam atlas, and 4-angle turntable runner | Settles frames, checks `findCoplanarOverlaps()` and `probeDepthConflicts()`, and saves valley, geometry-seam, 22-specimen turntable, and UI PNGs | `lib/shukuba/verify/frame_harness.dart` and `lib/shukuba/view/shukuba_page.dart` |
