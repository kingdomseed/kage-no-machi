## 2. Anti-Polygonal Geometry & Shader Rules

1. **No Raw Low-Poly Prisms or Faceted Terrain Edges:**
   - Replace all 8-sided/12-sided Dart prism rocks (`buildGorgeDressingNode`) with smooth-shaded level-3 subdivided sculpted GLBs (`rock_river_boulder.glb`, `rock_shore_riprap.glb`, `rock_cliff_a.glb`, `rock_cliff_b.glb`, `rock_coastal_headland.glb`).
   - Warp riverbanks (`sampleRawTerrain`) and non-quay Minato coves (`sampleMinatoRawTerrain`) with multi-harmonic organic displacement, shallow wet-cobble beaches (`dropSpan = 3.6 m`), and matching shader FBM domain-warping (`gouache_lit.fmat`, `river_water.fmat`, `harbor_water.fmat`).
   - Preserve exact walkable street elevation near bridge abutments (`|z| < 4.5 m`) so bridges and stone water-stairs seat cleanly.
2. **Smooth-Shaded Curvature on Architecture, Bridges, Ships & Flora:**
   - Use 12–32 spline/catmull segments along roof *terimukuri* gables, cambered bridge decks & railings, curved `sengokubune` hulls & billowed bamboo-ribbed sails, circular lantern hoops, wagon awnings, and Bezier tree trunks (`bmesh` `smooth = True` on rounded surfaces, hard creases only on intentional architectural beam edges).
3. **Zero Coplanar Overlays & Engine Hygiene:**
   - Offsetproud decorative trim, signage, windows, shingles, and dock planks by `>= 0.018 m` along outward normals.
   - Maintain `TerrainConfig.gridStep == 0.5`, single-chunk rebuild `< 4 ms`, `terrain_outer_apron_*` strictly outside `[-88, 88]`, and exact `skyFade` / `shadowAmbientStrength` invariants.
